# Evertas

> Evertas is a digital-asset insurance underwriter and the first company dedicated exclusively to crypto insurance. Founded in 2017 as BlockRe by J. Gdanski and Raymond Zenkich and rebranded to Evertas in 2020, it underwrites cryptocurrency mining and AI-infrastructure hardware property, platform failure, crime/theft/loss, insider theft/loss, digital property, and directors-and-officers risk. Products are underwritten by certain underwriters at Lloyd's of London and other insurers, with coverage limits up to $600 million per declaration. Policies are distributed exclusively through appointed brokers.

## API surface

Evertas publishes NO public API, developer portal, SDK, CLI, webhook catalog, or
machine-readable specification (OpenAPI, AsyncAPI, GraphQL SDL, Protobuf, MCP manifest,
or A2A agent card). This was verified on 2026-08-12 by walking the site's full
117-URL sitemap and probing every conventional contract and discovery path on
evertas.com and portal.evertas.com. Insurance is bound through appointed brokers,
not through a programmable interface.

The only application-like surface is a Brokers Portal at https://evertas.com/portal/,
which is a name/email form that emails back a secure link for uploading documents.
It exposes no API.

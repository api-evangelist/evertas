For those interested in the latest in insurtech, there is a compelling podcast series worth investigating called InsurTechTalk , hosted by Gilad Shai. These are 45 minute long conversations that flow very naturally and are easy to follow. Gilad starts the conversation asking about the guest’s career path and how it led them to be sitting opposite him at that moment, and everybody’s story alone is worth an episode.

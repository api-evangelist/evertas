Ryan Lackey is Chief Security Officer (CSO) at Evertas. He has been involved in cryptocurrency and computer security since the 1990s – participating on cypherpunks mailing list where many early ideas for electronic cash, trustworthy computing, censorship-resistant communications networks, and globally distributed systems were actively discussed and prototyped.

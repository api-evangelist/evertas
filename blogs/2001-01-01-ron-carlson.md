Ron Carlson is Chief Executive Officer at Evertas Insurance Agency LLC. Ron brings 35 years of [Re]Insurance experience to Evertas covering every spectrum from retail agency, Reinsurance (Managing Director with focus on property catastrophe), Carrier (CUO, operations, MGA management), MGA Executive, and founder of a property MGA.

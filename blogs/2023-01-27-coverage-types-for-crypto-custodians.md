There are four types of crypto insurance policies primarily used to mitigate the risks confronting custodians. Specie The kind of coverage typically applied to high-value, physical items, such as artwork, cash, gold, precious gems, and even valuable documents — while these items are in transit, in storage or on display. Anything that might be the goal of an Ocean’s 11-type heist is going to be covered by a specie policy.

In early June of 2023, it emerged that the decentralized Atomic Wallet software was hacked, and millions worth of funds stolen as a result. This is significant for many reasons, the primary being that the keys were taken from multiple individual users’ private devices, not from a centralized platform, which is how crypto theft has traditionally occurred. It is understood that the exploit was delivered via a software update tainted with malicious code.

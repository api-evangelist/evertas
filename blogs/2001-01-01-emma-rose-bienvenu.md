Emma Rose joins Evertas from Pantera Capital, where she spent several years as Chief of Staff working closely with the CIO and CEO to grow the firm and its team. She previously worked at Linklaters LLP, in London, and at Canadian investors CDPQ (with assets under management of more than $435 Billion) and PSP (with AUM of $243 Billion).

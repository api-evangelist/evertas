By Thomas Shewchuck As cryptocurrencies continue to gain traction globally, regulatory frameworks are emerging to bring structure to the rapidly expanding crypto market. One of the most notable advancements in this field is the European Union’s new Markets in Crypto-Assets (MiCA) regulation , designed to create a unified approach to crypto regulations across Europe.
